Fifty-eight explicitly describe an unbranded illustrative photograph rather than exact packaging. Any illustrative photograph used for a named-brand product must not imply that it depicts that product's exact packaging or an endorsement.

Five collection images record an Unsplash licence. The remaining four collection records have no image, source URL or licence (Religious, Feta, Festive and Olives).

## Owner decision and outstanding work

On 2026-10-31, the owner instructed us to **keep existing product images visible while supplier permissions are pending**. This is an operational decision, not a grant of rights, a finding of infringement, or an assurance that the images are commercially cleared. Product listings and publication states have not been changed by this review.

For the 419 published records with unresolved permission:

1. Request written e-commerce image permission, a supplier media-kit licence, or an authorised replacement from the manufacturer/distributor. A manufacturer or retailer hosting a photograph does not automatically license FreshCo to reuse it.
2. Alternatively use FreshCo's own photography, commissioned photography with appropriate rights, or suitable licensed stock clearly labelled as illustrative where accurate.
3. Retain the source URL, rights-holder name, permitted use, permission/terms link or evidence reference, and approval date in the admin permission notes. Do not store passwords or private credentials there.
4. Replace or remove an image if permission is refused or a rights holder requests it. Do not mark an unresolved record cleared merely because the image loads or visually matches.

The admin editor now preserves existing source/permission notes when a partial API update omits those fields. An explicitly submitted blank value intentionally clears the corresponding field.

## Primary references

- [Pexels licence](https://www.pexels.com/license/): permits use on websites and e-commerce shops subject to its restrictions, including no implied endorsement.
- [Unsplash licence](https://unsplash.com/license): permits commercial image use subject to its terms and exclusions.
- [Australian Attorney-General's Department — Copyright basics](https://www.ag.gov.au/rights-and-protections/copyright/copyright-basics): explains protection of photographs, owners' rights and exceptions.

This operational review is not legal advice and does not certify that every image is non-infringing. Specific rights questions should be resolved with the rights holder or qualified legal advice.
